What LifeFlow does

Built around four concrete objectives

01

Secure registration & login

Separate, secure portals for donors and blood bank administrators, so records stay accurate on both sides.

02

Map-based search

Locate nearby blood banks or active donation drives on a map, filtered by distance and blood type.

03

Slot-based booking

A booking calendar lets donors reserve a donation time, cutting down long wait times at clinics.

04

Emergency request module

Hospitals and individuals can broadcast an urgent need for a specific blood type to nearby donors.
